# Code of Conduct

Ingle is made by and for people who like building things. Keep it a place where everyone feels welcome.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work rather than the person, welcome
newcomers and questions, and credit other people's ideas.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make jokes or comments about anyone's gender, gender identity,
sexuality, race, religion, disability, age, appearance or background; share anyone's private information;
or keep pushing after someone has asked you to stop.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you're representing the project.

**If something's wrong:** contact the maintainer privately (via the profile linked in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)).
Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block people who break
this code, and will explain why where it's safe to.
